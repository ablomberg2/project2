About This Project

We created this project to explore how image classification and machine learning work. Our model was trained with original photographs of keys and coins collected by our group.

The model looks for patterns in the images it was trained on and uses those patterns to make predictions. Its predictions are not always guaranteed to be correct. Differences in lighting, backgrounds, and camera angles can affect its results.
