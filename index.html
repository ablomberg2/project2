Implicit Bias

Understanding bias, identity, and intersectionality

This website explores implicit bias, the Implicit Association Test, and intersectionality so we can better understand how our identities and experiences shape our perspectives.

Explore Resources About This Project

Why Does This Matter?

Implicit bias is defined as an unconscious attitude, belief, or stereotype about a social group that influences how a person perceives and behaves toward others without realizing it. We can learn about these ideas, which can help us think about where our beliefs come from and how they may affect others.

This website is designed as an introduction for college students who are new to the terms implicit bias, the IAT, and intersectionality. The goal of this website is to encourage curiosity, reflection, and continued learning.
