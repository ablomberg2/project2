If these topics are new to you, you do not need to know everything at once. These resources are meant to help students move from simply learning definitions to thinking about how bias and identity can affect people's everyday experiences.

Questions to Think About

Notice

What assumptions do you notice yourself making about people before getting to know them? How might those assumptions affect your interactions with others?

Question

Where might that assumption have come from? How might it be different if you had a different identity or life experience?

Reflect

How could different parts of someone's identity change the way they experience the same situation?
