About Us

Our goal is to make conversations about implicit bias and intersectionality easier to understand.

Why We Created This Site

Implicit bias can affect the way people think about and respond to others without them always realizing it. Learning about these biases gives us an opportunity to think more carefully about where our assumptions come from and how they can affect other people.

Intersectionality

Intersectionality is important because people do not experience the world through only one part of their identity. Factors such as sexuality, gender, race, class, and other identities can overlap and influence someone's experiences.

Implicit Bias

Implicit biases are automatic associations or assumptions that can influence our thoughts and behavior. Having an implicit bias does not automatically mean that someone consciously agrees with that bias.

One reason we think learning about implicit bias is useful is because recognizing our automatic reactions can give us a chance to question them. The goal is not to assume that one test can completely define someone, but rather to use these experiences as an opportunity for reflection.
